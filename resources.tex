\subsection{Accommodations for Students with Disabilities}
 
If you have an accommodations letter from the Office of Disability Resources, 
please discuss your accommodations and needs with me as early in the semester as possible so we can make sure they're in place. 
If you suspect you may have a disability and would benefit from accommodations but are not yet registered, 
contact the Office of Disability Resources directly: \url{https://www.cmu.edu/disability-resources/} (access@andrew.cmu.edu).
 
\subsection{Statement of Support for Students’ Health \& Well-being}
 
Take care of yourself. If you or anyone you know is experiencing academic stress, difficult life events, 
or feelings of anxiety or depression, please reach out for support sooner rather than later --- 
to a friend, to me, or to Counseling and Psychological Services (CaPS): 412-268-2922, \url{http://www.cmu.edu/counseling/}.

\subsection{Diversity Statement}
 
We must treat every individual with respect. 
Diversity --- of race, color, national origin, language, sex, disability, age, sexual orientation, 
gender identity, religion, creed, ancestry, belief, veteran status, genetic information, and more --- 
is fundamental to building and maintaining an equitable and inclusive campus community, 
and each of us is responsible for creating a safer, more inclusive environment. 
See CMU's full diversity and inclusion statement and resources at \url{https://www.cmu.edu/inclusive-excellence/}.
 
If you experience or observe unfair or hostile treatment on the basis of identity, please speak out; 
the \textbf{Center for Student Diversity and Inclusion} (csdi@andrew.cmu.edu, (412) 268-2150) is here to help.
 
\subsection{Ethics Reporting Hotline}
 
To report suspected unethical or illegal activity related to academic and student life, financial matters, 
human relations, or campus safety --- confidentially or anonymously --- use CMU's Ethics Reporting Hotline, 
operated by a third party (NAVEX): call 1-844-587-0793 or visit \url{https://cmu.ethicspoint.com}. 
This hotline is \emph{not} an emergency service; for emergencies, call CMU Police (412-268-2323) or 911.

\subsection{Accommodations for Students with Disabilities}
If you have an accommodations letter from the Office of Disability Resources, 
please discuss your accommodations and needs with me as early in the semester as possible so we can make sure they're in place. 
If you suspect you may have a disability and would benefit from accommodations but are not yet registered, 
contact the Office of Disability Resources directly: \url{cmu.edu/disability-resources/} (access@andrew.cmu.edu).
 
\subsection{Statement of Support for Students’ Health \& Well-being}
\textbf{Take care of yourself.} If you or anyone you know is experiencing academic stress, difficult life events, 
or feelings of anxiety or depression, please reach out for support sooner rather than later --- 
to a friend, to me, or to CaPS.
 
 \begin{tcolorbox}[colback=blue!5,colframe=blue!75!black]
If you or someone you know is feeling suicidal or in danger of self-harm, call someone immediately, day or night:\\
 
Counseling \& Psychological Services (CaPS): 412-268-2922, \url{cmu.edu/counseling/}\\
Suicide and Crisis Lifeline: 988 \hspace*{5em} Re:solve Crisis Network: 888-796-8226\\ 
 
If the situation is life threatening, call the police:\\ 
412-268-2323 (on campus) or 911 (off campus)
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Inclusivity}
We must treat every individual with respect. 
Diversity (race, color, national origin, language, sex, disability, age, sexual orientation, 
gender identity, religion, ancestry, belief, veteran status, genetic information, etc.)
is fundamental to building and maintaining an inclusive campus community; 
each of us is responsible for creating a safer, more inclusive environment. 
See CMU's full inclusivity resources at \url{cmu.edu/inclusive-excellence/}.
 
If you experience or observe unfair or hostile treatment on the basis of identity,  speak to 
the \textbf{Center for Student Diversity and Inclusion} (csdi@andrew..., (412) 268-2150).
 
\subsection{Ethics Reporting Hotline}
To report suspected unethical or illegal activity related to academic and student life, financial matters, 
human relations, or campus safety --- confidentially or anonymously --- use CMU's Ethics Reporting Hotline, 
operated by a third party (NAVEX): call 1-844-587-0793 or visit \url{cmu.ethicspoint.com}. 
\textbf{This hotline is \emph{not} an emergency service}.%; for emergencies, call CMU Police (412-268-2323) or 911.
